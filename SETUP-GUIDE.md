# Midland Estate & Builders: Phase 1 setup (no software needed)

## 1. Put the files on GitHub (free)
1. Unzip `midland-estate.zip` on your computer.
2. Go to github.com, sign up, then click **New repository**. Name it `midland-estate`, keep it Private, click **Create repository**.
3. Click **uploading an existing file**. Open the unzipped folder and drag ALL its contents (the `src` and `public` folders and every file) into the page.
4. Click **Commit changes**.

## 2. Deploy on Vercel (free)
1. Go to vercel.com and sign up with your GitHub account.
2. Click **Add New > Project**, choose `midland-estate`, then click **Deploy**.
3. After about 2 minutes you get a live link. Open it and check the site.

## 3. Updating later
Each time I give you changed files, open the repository on GitHub, click **Add file > Upload files**, drop them in (same folder paths), and commit. Vercel redeploys automatically.

Note: Buy, Rent, Sell, Projects, About, Contact, Privacy and Terms pages arrive in later phases. Their menu links show the 404 page until then.
